Weekly Releases
Friday 30 October 2026
Hip-hop led the week, with Shabazz Palaces' It's a Style Sport on top at a 7.0 average review score and several other rap albums arriving with little coverage beyond release news. Pitchfork reviewed DIIV, Huerco S., Tony Iommi & Jørn Lande and Dead Deities, and Stereogum named DIIV its Album Of The Week. Much of the rest was singles and album announcements from Everything But The Girl, The Flaming Lips, Junior Boys and others.
