Weekly Releases
Friday 30 October 2026
This week's coverage was split between a few album reviews and a long list of single and album announcements. Pitchfork and The Needle Drop reviewed Shabazz Palaces, while Pitchfork also covered Huerco S. and Tony Iommi with Jørn Lande, and Stereogum named DIIV its Album Of The Week. Most of the singles came with little more than a headline, so several entries below are short.
Top of the week
1
Shabazz Palaces – It's a Style Sport
Album · Hip-hop / R&B · Wed 28 Oct
♫ Similar to Armand Hammer, Billy Woods
Shabazz Palaces released the album It's a Style Sport on October 28. The Needle Drop calls it the group's strongest showing since their first couple of albums. Pitchfork describes it as a shadowy and uncharacteristically introspective record in which Ishmael Butler explores the cosmos within.
The Needle Drop video review  |  Pitchfork review
2
Huerco S. – From the Toolkit of Lovers
Album · Electronic / Dance
♫ Similar to Oneohtrix Point Never, Andy Stott
Pitchfork reviewed From the Toolkit of Lovers, a sprawling triple LP from Huerco S. It says the album nudges the minimal-house revival toward new extremes of detail, duration and sensuousness, and calls the grooves hypnotic and late-night. No release date was listed.

4
Tony Iommi & Jørn Lande – From the Dark
Album · Rock / Alternative / Indie
Pitchfork reviewed From the Dark, a new solo album from the 78-year-old Black Sabbath guitarist Tony Iommi, made with Norwegian vocalist Jørn Lande. Pitchfork says it returns to heavy-metal basics and calls it proudly uncompromising. No release date was listed.
