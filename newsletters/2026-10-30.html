Weekly Releases
Friday 30 October 2026
Reviews led the week, with Pitchfork and The Needle Drop covering albums from Shabazz Palaces, Tony Iommi and Jørn Lande, and Huerco S., while Stereogum named DIIV's ZIRP! its Album of the Week. The rest of the list was mostly singles and announcements, including a Flaming Lips album announcement and the first new Dead Man's Bones music in 17 years.
Top of the week
1
Shabazz Palaces – It's a Style Sport
Album · Hip-hop / R&B · Out Wed 28 Oct
The Needle Drop reviewed this album and called it Shabazz Palaces' strongest showing since their first couple of albums. Pitchfork also reviewed it, describing Ishmael Butler as exploring the cosmos within on a shadowy and uncharacteristically introspective record. It came out October 28.
